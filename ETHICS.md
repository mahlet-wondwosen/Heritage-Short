# ETHICS

## Consent

No real person's face, likeness or voice appears in this film. The grandmother is an illustrated figure shown from behind, in profile or in silhouette. The narration uses a stock Gemini TTS voice, not a cloned voice. Because nobody real is shown or voiced, no consent record is needed for the film itself.

The story comes from my own family's coffee ceremony tradition. I showed the film to my mother Tshehay, my father Wondwosen and my brother Teddy, and they agreed to it being made and shared.

## Labelling

- The film ends with a 3 second card: "Made with AI. A family story."
- The post is published with the platform's AI-content disclosure switched on.
- Watermarks and provenance metadata added by the tools are left in place.

## Private data

## Cultural care

The Amharic narration was drafted with AI help and then proofread by me, a native speaker, before the final render. The ceremony is shown with respect: no mockery, no invented rituals.
